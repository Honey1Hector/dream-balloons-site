About Dream Balloons

Breathtaking Displays

Dream Balloons creates elegant, bespoke balloon displays designed to make your celebrations truly magical. Based in the East End of Newcastle, we proudly serve the surrounding areas and are happy to travel for your special occasion. Together, we can design a completely bespoke display, from floral arrangements to breathtaking custom colours. Every detail is thoughtfully considered. We are passionate about staying ahead of the latest interior design trends to exploring seasonal colour palettes.

At Dream Balloons, we believe the magic is in the details. Working closely with you, we bring your vision to life, creating a statement display that feels beautifully considered, effortlessly elegant and truly unforgettable. Every display is tailored to your event, from the colour palette and balloon finishes to the size, shape and finishing details.
